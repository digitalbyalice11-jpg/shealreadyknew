step i

pull your chart

One free website, about three minutes. Have your date of birth, your exact time of birth, and the town you were born in. It's usually on your birth certificate.

Go to IHDS — free human design chart. Under the NEWCOMERS tab click “Free human design chart.”
Fill in your birth date, exact time and city.
Write down your TYPE and your AUTHORITY. That's all you need — everything else on that page is a rabbit hole.

No birth time? Tick the box below and you'll still get most of it.
