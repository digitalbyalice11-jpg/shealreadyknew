1

pull your chart

Go to IHDS — free human design chart. Under the NEWCOMERS tab click “Free human design chart.” Enter your birth date, exact time and city.

Write down two things: your TYPE and your AUTHORITY. That's it. Everything else on that page is a rabbit hole you don't need.

Takes about three minutes. It's free. No card.

2

read book one — find your current

Short read. Four currents, four ways of creating. Read all four — one of them will read like someone opened your diary. That's yours.

If two feel true, pick the one that describes a bad week rather than a good one. That's the more accurate read.

3

open your alignment reading

Pick your birth city from the list — it sets your coordinates and timezone for you. Add the type and authority you just wrote down, your current from book one, and your business info.

You’ll need your Astro-Seek data export. The page shows you exactly where to get it — about a minute. It’s required, and it’s the reason your reading comes back specific instead of vague.

Don't skip the “your voice” section. Three quick boxes: what people ask you, what you believe that others don't, and one thing you've been through that's finished. That section is the difference between a reading that sounds like you and one that sounds like a template.

two things worth knowing

before you start

Your exact birth time matters. An hour off can change your rising sign and your profile. It's usually on your birth certificate. Worth the five minutes it takes to find.

Don't know your birth time? Tick the box that says so. You'll still get your sun, your nodes and your whole content direction — the parts that matter most here. The pieces that genuinely need a time get left out rather than guessed at, because a confident wrong answer is worse than a blank.

Bookmark both links. You can run them as many times as you like — when your offer changes, when your goal changes, when you finish your 14 days and want the next 14, or for a friend.
