step i

pull your chart

Two free websites, about four minutes total. Have three things ready: your date of birth, your exact time of birth, and the town you were born in. The exact time matters — an hour off can change your result. It's usually on your birth certificate.

1 · your human design chart
Go to IHDS — free human design chart. Look under the NEWCOMERS tab and click “Free human design chart.”
Fill out the little questionnaire — birth date, exact time, and city.
On your chart, find TYPE — Generator, Manifesting Generator, Projector, Manifestor, or Reflector. Then find your AUTHORITY (Sacral, Emotional, Splenic…). Select both in step ii below.
That is everything you need from this chart. Close the tab. Everything else on it is a rabbit hole you do not need to fall into to make money this month.
2 · your astrology data (required)
Go to Astro-Seek — birth natal chart calculator, fill in your birth info, and click Calculate chart.
Scroll down — on the right, in the Quick Tabs list, there’s a link that says “AI-ChatGPT – Astrology Data Export.” Click it, then press Copy to grab the whole block.
Paste it into the box in step ii. Don't skip this one. This page works out your sun, moon, rising and nodes on its own — but the export carries every placement, house and aspect, and that's what stops your reading coming back vague. Same effort every time, same depth every time.
